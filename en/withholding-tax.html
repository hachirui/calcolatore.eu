Frequently Asked Questions
What is withholding tax?+
A tax withheld at source by the payer on behalf of the payee and paid directly to the tax authority.
Who is subject to withholding tax?+
Self-employed professionals providing services to businesses are typically subject to withholding tax.
How to show it on an invoice?+
Show gross fee, add VAT, deduct withholding tax. Client pays: gross + VAT - withholding.
